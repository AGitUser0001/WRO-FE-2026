# Electronics interfaces

Physical evidence of vehicle electronics interfaces used by the system-architecture documentation.

- `tm171-imu-installation-highlighted.png`: TM171 IMU location inside the vehicle.
- `imu-usb-c-interface-closeup.jpeg`: close-up of the IMU USB-C interface.
- `imu-usb-c-to-jetson-usb-a-overview.jpeg`: wider view of the IMU-to-Jetson USB connection.
- `imu-usb-c-port.jpeg`: IMU USB-C port.
- `lidar-2-usb-dupont-cable.jpeg`: LiDAR 2 cable detail.
- `front-lidar-zhr4-interface.jpeg` and `front-lidar-zhr4-to-gpio-dupont.jpeg`: front-LiDAR ZHR-4 interface and its Dupont-jumper connection to Jetson GPIO.
- `rear-lidar-zhr4-interface.jpeg`, `rear-lidar-zhr4-cp2102-rx-to-usb-a.jpeg`, and `cp2102-usb-a-rx-interface.jpeg`: rear-LiDAR ZHR-4 interface and its CP2102-to-Jetson USB-A connection.
- `w5500-to-esp32-dupont-jumpers.jpeg`: close-up of W5500-to-ESP32 Dupont jumpers.
- `w5500-integrated-wiring-01.jpeg` and `w5500-integrated-wiring-02.jpeg`: broader W5500 wiring views.
- `w5500-jetson-ethernet-esp32-dupont-overview.jpeg`: W5500 Ethernet and Dupont-jumper overview.
